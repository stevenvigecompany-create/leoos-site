Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

What we collect

When you book a call or email us, we get your name, email, business details you choose to share, and the call notes. During a setup, your operator runs inside your own accounts (email, calendar, tools); we access them only with your permission, only to do the setup and care work you asked for.

How we use it

To deliver and support the service and to reply to you. We don't sell your information or your clients' information, and we don't use your business data to train AI models. AI providers we use process data under their own terms.

Your choices

You can revoke our access to any account at any time from that account's settings, and ask us to delete your records at leoos@mail.grokbot.com. Texas residents have the right to access, correct, and delete their personal data.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.
